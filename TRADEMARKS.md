# Trademarks and brand assets

Gradient is open source, but the brand it documents is not.

## What the MIT licence covers

[LICENSE](LICENSE) covers everything that is code:

- the design tokens (`tokens.json` and the CSS, Tailwind and JSON built from it)
- the stylesheets and the scripts
- the Shades interface icons in `packages/gradient/icons/`
- the website's source

You may use, copy, change and sell work built with them, in any project, with or without credit.

## What it does not cover

These belong to **Shades Media Group** and are not licensed for reuse:

- **The name.** "Shades" and "Shades Media Group" as names of a business, product or service.
- **The mark and the lockup.** The red square with three white bars, and the SHADES wordmark: every file in `packages/gradient/brand/` whose name starts with `shades-`, and every image of them.
- **The Spectra family.** Spectra, Mera, Sol, Nova, Sage, Vio and Sunny: the files in `packages/gradient/brand/spectra/`, their names, and any drawing that copies their construction (the three-bar crest on a soft single-colour body).

These are in the repository because Shades products and the projects Shades builds for its clients need them. They ship in the npm package for the same reason.

## You may

- Link to Shades, show the mark next to a link to shades.md, or describe Shades in an article or review, without changing the mark.
- Say a product was "built by Shades Media Group" when Shades built it.
- Build your own product with the tokens, styles and icons, under your own name and logo.

## Please don't

- Use the mark, the wordmark or a Spectra character in your own product, logo, merchandise or marketing.
- Ship a product that looks like it comes from Shades when it does not.
- Make a modified version of the mark or the characters.

## Third-party marks

The service marks in `brandIcons` (GitHub, Figma, npm and others) come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) and belong to their owners. Use them only to link to those services.

Eight ideas

Eight different ways to say Shades in sound, made the way a foley artist works: a voice, a breath, the mouth, paper, glass, bottles, a felt piano. Each keeps the three notes at the mark's strengths, then is finished like a studio take: human timing, tape warmth, EQ, a real-sized room. Pick one, and the long intro and the product sounds are built from it.

Every sound, the voices and objects included, is synthesised from code in the gradient repo (packages/gradient/brand/sound/ideas.py and render.py), so we own all of it. Levelled to −16 LUFS with true peak under −1 dBTP. The shape beside each is its waveform.
